Asteroid occultations

An asteroid passes in front of a star. Its shadow sweeps a narrow path across the Earth: inside it the star blinks out for a few seconds, a few kilometres outside nothing happens.

  • You need a telescope of 100 mm or more, ideally a camera
  • How often two or three paths over India a night
  • Each page a month of paths across India, each mapped, with how close it passes you

How these are made

Everything here is computed, not copied. Sources: JPL (planets, Moon, Sun, the moons, asteroids), Gaia DR3 and Hipparcos-2 (stars), NASA's LRO laser altimeter (the Moon's mountains).

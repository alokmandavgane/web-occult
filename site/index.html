How these are made

Every time, line and diagram here is computed, not copied: planets, the Moon and the Sun from the JPL DE431 ephemeris, Jupiter's and Saturn's moons from JPL's satellite ephemerides, stars from Gaia DR3 and Hipparcos-2, and the Moon's mountains from NASA's LRO laser altimeter. The lunar-occultation list is chosen by hand; each page links the data it was built from.

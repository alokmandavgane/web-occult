The gold line is the north limit: along it Alcyone grazes the Moon's northern edge. North of the line it is a near miss — from Dwarka Alcyone passes just 0.1′ outside the limb — and a graze seen from right on the line, Alcyone blinking in and out behind lunar mountains, is the finest view of all. How to watch

Method & accuracy

Positions from the JPL DE431 ephemeris (Skyfield), ΔT = 69.1 s. The city table's contact times and the solid limit line use the real lunar limb — the silhouette of LRO LOLA terrain (LDEM_64, ~0.5 km) at each observer's own libration — so they carry the mountains and valleys that a mean sphere misses (up to a few seconds at ordinary contacts, tens of seconds near a graze). The any-location solver in your browser and the shaded region still use the mean sphere of 1737.4 km (±2 s). Alcyone's own disc (0″ across) is included: partial contacts are the from/to times.

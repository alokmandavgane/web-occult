The numbers

Everything here is computed for the place in the chip above: the time is when the shadow passes closest to you, and the chord and fade are what you would record there. The asteroid is a sphere of its catalogue diameter, so a real, lumpy one casts a slightly different shadow — which is exactly what timing it from several places measures. Once you have opened this page it stays on your phone — the times, the charts, the path and the map tiles you looked at — so it still works where you are going, which is usually somewhere without a signal.

Closest approach 14:19 UTC (geocentric, 0.92° centre to centre) · Moon 8.6 days old, 68% lit, 112° from the Sun

Where it can be seen

sees the occultation after sunset graze limit city sees it near miss

The gold line is the north limit: along it Jupiter grazes the Moon's northern edge. North of the line it is a near miss — from Lubumbashi, CD Jupiter passes just 2.4′ outside the limb — and a graze seen from right on the line, Jupiter blinking in and out behind lunar mountains, is the finest view of all. The solid line is traced against the real lunar limb — LRO LOLA terrain, the mountains and valleys that are on the edge for this libration — and sits -0.9 to +2.6 km from the mean-sphere line (dotted).

The violet shading is where the Sun has set by the time of the event, so the reappearance happens in a darkening sky.
